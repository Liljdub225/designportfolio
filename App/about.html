About Me

Hi, I'm Josh — a graphic and UX/UI designer passionate about creating meaningful visual and user experiences. My work blends curiosity, precision, and a drive to make lasting impact on people and brands. I specialize in both print and digital design, with experience in Adobe Creative Suite, web design, and brand identity.

Recently, I've expanded into front-end development using tools like Visual Studio Code. I'm excited by how design and interactivity intersect — and how these tools enhance the creative process. As AI becomes a bigger part of the design world, I've made it a point to understand how it can be used to work smarter, not harder. I'm committed to staying ahead of the curve by learning how to integrate AI into the creative workflow while staying rooted in what makes design human.
