Concordia University Wisconsin Projects

During my time at Concordia, I had the privilege of serving as the student graphic designer for the Center for Academic Advising and Career Engagement (CAACE) and the Marketing Department. In these roles, I was responsible for creating and updating a variety of digital and print materials for events both on and off campus.


This experience taught me how to maintain visual consistency across multiple formats, adapt designs to different media, and manage projects. It also strengthened my ability to work efficiently under deadlines while ensuring each piece aligned with the event's messaging and branding.
